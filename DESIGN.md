# Design System: Cream White & Green (Bright & Happening)

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual Identity & Metaphor
**Opportunity Earth - Bright Cream & Emerald Venture Control Room**
A clean, energetic, bright and happening interface in warm Cream White (`#fdfcf7`) and Rich Emerald Green (`#059669` / `#15803d`), featuring a 30%/60%/10% tri-pane layout and a character-driven AI assistant named **Radar Scout**.

## Absolute Palette Rules
- **PRIMARY THEME**: Warm Cream White (`#fdfcf7`, `#f4f0ea`) + Forest & Emerald Green (`#15803d`, `#059669`, `#166534`).
- **BANNED BACKGROUND/THEME COLORS**: No blue or purple backgrounds, panels, or primary buttons. (Blue/purple may only appear in literal word labels or secondary badge text when explicitly required).

## Color Tokens

### Base & Backgrounds
- **Cream White Ground**: `#fdfcf7`
- **Warm Paper Panel**: `#f5f2ea`
- **Card Surface**: `#ffffff` (pure white with green border)
- **Border Hairline**: `rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.18)`

### Rich Green & Warm Accents
- **Emerald Green Primary**: `#059669`
- **Forest Green Deep**: `#15803d`
- **Mint Glow**: `#a7f3d0`
- **Solar Amber / Gold**: `#d97706`
- **Deep Ink Text**: `#111827`
- **Muted Sage Text**: `#4b5563`

## Spatial Layout Allocation (30% / 60% / 10%)
- **Left Column (30% Screen Width)**: Features & Opportunity Signals Section. When a card/feature is clicked, detail view slides into this 30% space without obscuring the world map!
- **Center Column (60% Screen Width)**: Standard World Map Stage. High-clarity 2D/3D map showing exact global location nodes being exploited.
- **Right Column (10% Screen Width / Docked Rail)**: "Radar Scout" Character AI Chatbot with expandable assistant panel.

## Character AI Chatbot: "Radar Scout"
- **Name**: Radar Scout 🧭 (Friendly, quick-witted, cheerful venture guide)
- **Avatar**: Animated scout badge with pulsing compass and joyful expressions
- **Voice**: Conversational, encouraging, sharp, and helpful with Swiggy/Zomato style quick-action chips.
