# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Cobe / Leaflet / SVG Standard World Map, Lucide Icons, PostgreSQL / GDELT AI Opportunity Engine.

## Users
Broad audience ranging from executive investors and enterprise strategists to tech innovators, product builders, and curious global explorers tracking emerging world signals and market opportunities.

## Product Purpose
Antigravity Opportunity Radar ingests real-time GDELT global news streams, clusters event signals into story threads, and leverages AI models to synthesize actionable business, innovation, and investment opportunities across global markets.

## Positioning
A bright, happening, real-time intelligence control center rendered in a clean Cream White and Organic Green visual aesthetic, featuring a 30%/60%/10% tri-pane layout (30% Features/Signals, 60% Interactive Standard World Map, 10% Character-Driven AI Assistant).

## Operating Context
Used in web browsers on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Screen layout allocates 30% to Features & Opportunity Dossiers, 60% to the Standard World Map, and 10% to the Character AI Chatbot ("Radar Scout").

## Capabilities and Constraints
- High-throughput GDELT event stream parsing and geographic mapping.
- AI story clustering and viability scoring (Business, Innovation, Investment).
- Interactive Standard World Map visualizer with real-time location pins.
- Slide-over / Split Feature Inspector (30% Features Pane expands seamlessly to show full dossier without covering the map).
- Character-driven AI assistant ("Radar Scout") with distinct personality, fun micro-interactions, and instant Q&A.
- Must NOT use blue or purple as primary theme/background colors (Cream White & Vibrant Green only).

## Brand Commitments
- Palette: Warm Cream White (`#fcfbf7`), Soft Paper Surface (`#f4f0ea`), Rich Emerald/Forest Green (`#15803d`, `#059669`, `#166534`), Solar Gold (`#d97706`), Warm Terracotta/Amber accents.
- Bright and happening tone: Energetic, clear, inviting, and human.
- 30% / 60% / 10% tri-pane responsive spatial allocation.
- Character AI Chatbot: "Radar Scout" (a friendly, quick-witted global guide with custom avatar & personality).

## Evidence on Hand
- Working Next.js App Router codebase (`app/page.tsx`, `app/globals.css`, `components/globe/`, `components/dashboard/`, `components/layout/`).
- GDELT ingestion scripts, opportunity synthesis engine, relevance scoring, and mock fallbacks.

## Product Principles
1. Bright & Happening Polish: High-contrast cream & green aesthetic that feels alive, fresh, and delightful.
2. Spatial Clarity: Fixed 30/60/10 split so map, signals, and AI assistant are always accessible side-by-side.
3. Unobstructed Navigation: Clicking any feature slides open detail inspection in the 30% features column without hiding the 60% world map.
4. Delightful Character Guidance: "Radar Scout" AI provides warm, conversational, character-rich intelligence.
